# Pathfinder AI - Labor Market Dataset Design

## Purpose

The seed_roles.csv dataset acts as the initial labor market knowledge base for Pathfinder AI.

Each row represents one career role.

The dataset stores structured market intelligence such as required skills, salary benchmarks, market demand, competition, preparation effort, and work characteristics.

It serves as the primary source of truth for the Reality Engine, Career Decision Engine, Career Verdict Engine, analytics dashboards, and future machine learning experiments.
